About the Project

Our Vision & Inspiration

Pin and Explore was born out of a shared curiosity about the physical remnants of the past. Living in the Philippines, we are constantly reminded that landmarks like Fort Santiago and Baluarte de San Diego are far more than old stones—they are vessels for the stories of the people who lived, fought, and dreamed there.

We realized that every country has these foundational echoes, from the Colosseum in Rome to ancient fortresses across the globe. Our goal with this project was to bridge the digital gap between these monumental structures and users eager to learn their secrets, making global heritage accessible without requiring a passport or a plane ticket.

Academic & Development Context

This website was engineered as a collaborative group project by four developers for Intro to Computing (CC 100), CS1105 | Web Development Project.

The primary objective of this development cycle was to successfully integrate interactive UI elements with a structured historical database, creating a seamless, educational user experience.

The Tech Stack

To bring the interactive map and historical profiles to life, our team utilized the following technologies:

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS
  • Assets: Assets from credited sources for managing and serving country-specific historical descriptions and images.
  • Version Control: Git and GitHub for collaborative repository management and deployment.
